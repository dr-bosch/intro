Structural Antimony

A nineteen-year record of formal submissions, formal acknowledgements, and formal silence —explained in plain terms, for everyone.

B
Dr Bosch · The Meinhardt Initiative
Systems Scientist · Independent Research · Operations Research for the Public Good

I study how institutions handle knowledge. This record is the clearest case I have encountered of a system that receives information, produces the appearance of processing it, and structurally prevents any outcome from occurring. I am publishing it because the public is the constituency that was harmed.

What this is

In May 2006, a formally structured corpus of Operations Research (decision science, logistics optimisation, emergency planning models) was submitted to Irish government departments. It had documented applications to national security, energy grid management, and emergency response. The submitter followed every procedure available to a citizen operating in good faith.

Nineteen years later, not one department has issued a determination. The corpus has never been evaluated, rejected, or returned. It has been acknowledged, deferred, referred, redirected, and eventually ignored — by six departments, four governments, and every parliamentary committee that received it.

This is not a bureaucratic backlog. It is a documented structural condition: a system that converts formal engagement into administrative silence as its normal operating output. The record names it the Nullification Loop. What it means in plain terms is described below.

Operations Research is the discipline governments use to decide how to allocate ambulances, how to price electricity, how to route emergency supplies when a storm knocks out the grid. Ireland held a proprietary corpus of these methods unexamined while Storm Éowyn overwhelmed the national emergency response in January 2025. The technology to model exactly that kind of failure was in State custody. It arrived without it.

Baby logic · Five moments

What a child would see — and what the record shows

Each moment below starts with the simplest possible observation. Then the record is stated precisely.

01 · Receipt without response
They got my letter. Why didn't they answer?
Every department issued a reference number. That reference number is an acknowledgement of receipt. It creates a legal obligation to determine the matter — to evaluate it, refuse it with reasons, or identify who should. None of the six departments that acknowledged the submission has ever done any of those three things. The reference number is where the State's engagement ends and the Nullification Loop begins.
§ Article 41 CFR — right to good administration: impartial, fair handling within a reasonable time; duty to give reasons. Meadows v Minister for Justice [2010] 2 IR 701.
02 · Everyone says ask someone else
Everyone says try the next door. No one is behind the next door.
The Office of Emergency Planning said Operations Research was outside its remit in 2009. In 2025 it said Operations Research was already in use everywhere. Both statements closed the file. Both cannot be true. The Department of Foreign Affairs held the EU competent authority designation for dual-use goods for eighteen years and issued zero assessments. When the designation transferred to a new department in 2024, no record of the unprocessed submission transferred with it. The new department holds the obligation without knowing it exists.
§ O'Keeffe v An Bord Pleanála [1993] 1 IR 39 — irrationality: no reasonable decision-maker could maintain two mutually exclusive positions as grounds for the same non-determination. EU Regulation 2021/821.
03 · The person who promised forgot
He said he would fix it. He is in charge now. He still hasn't fixed it.
In February 2009, the current Taoiseach — then Minister for Foreign Affairs — received a four-and-a-half-hour technical presentation and committed in writing to producing a ministerial report. On 25 September 2009, he gave a verbal commitment at the submitter's residence to review the dual-use goods dimension. He has since held the offices of Taoiseach, Tánaiste, and Taoiseach again. The report has never been produced. No account of its non-production has ever been given. The period from the 2009 commitment to today is approximately seventeen and a half years.
§ Article 28.4.1 Bunreacht na hÉireann — Government collectively responsible to the Dáil for all executive action. Heaney v Ireland [1994] 3 IR 593 — proportionality applied to executive non-action.
04 · They threw the letter away
They said they didn't want to read it. Then they threw it away so no one else could.
In October 2025, the Joint Committee on European Union Affairs formally declined the submission — and then deleted the correspondence from its records. No statutory basis for deletion was cited. No transmission to a competent body was made. A parliamentary committee destroyed formally lodged EU compliance correspondence without any procedural account. This is not an administrative irregularity. Article 41 of the EU Charter guarantees every person the right of access to their own file. Deletion is a direct violation of that guarantee on its face.
§ Article 41 CFR — right of access to file. Article 4(3) TEU — sincere cooperation: all state organs must ensure fulfilment of treaty obligations. Ethics in Public Office Act 1995, §8(3).
05 · Ireland is now in charge of the rule
They're supposed to be in charge of the rule they're breaking.
Ireland holds the EU Council Presidency — including the chair of working groups governing the exact dual-use export control regulation the original submission engaged. The EU Commission's mandatory evaluation of that regulation opens on 10 September 2026. That evaluation cannot be filtered by any Irish government department. It will examine Ireland's competent authority record. That record, across four transposition cycles and nineteen years, is zero determinations. The Commission will receive this record as a fact, not a complaint.
§ EU Regulation 2021/821, Article 26 — mandatory statistical reporting to the Commission. Article 2 TEU — Rule of Law as foundational value. Ireland EU Council Presidency — 1 July 2026.
The record in three questions

What the record requires an answer to

These are not rhetorical. Each has a specific legal anchor. Each remains open.

01
Was the corpus evaluated?
The six departments that acknowledged receipt have never confirmed evaluation, rejection with reasons, or referral to a competent body. The answer, across nineteen years, is that it was not. That answer is the basis for mandamus — a court order compelling performance of a legal duty refused or neglected.
02
Who holds the obligation now?
The competent authority designation transferred from the Department of Foreign Affairs to a new department in August 2024 without audit, without transmission of the accumulated obligation, and without institutional memory. The answer is: three departments hold fragments of the function; none holds the whole obligation; no statutory coordination mechanism exists between them.
03
What is the cost of non-evaluation?
EirGrid has never been benchmarked against the submitted grid-optimisation models. The emergency logistics framework was not deployed during Storm Éowyn. Climate targets are being set without quantitative compatibility assessment of simultaneous road-expansion funding. The cost is not speculative. It is compounding annually.
